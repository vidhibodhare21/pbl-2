About citrus canker

Citrus canker is a bacterial disease that can affect leaves, twigs and fruit. Similar-looking marks can also have other causes, so a photo-based impression should not be treated as a confirmed diagnosis.

Signs to check

  • Small raised, tan-to-brown spots that may become corky.
  • Yellow or water-soaked-looking margins around spots; check both leaf surfaces.
  • Similar lesions on young twigs or fruit may add context, but need expert assessment.

Spread and conditions

The bacteria can spread with wind-driven rain and contaminated plant material or tools. Avoid handling wet plants, and clean tools between plants. Follow local plant-health guidance, especially before moving citrus plants.

Practical next steps Photograph the whole plant and close-ups of both leaf sides. Compare several leaves, note whether symptoms are spreading, and ask a local horticulture or agriculture extension specialist for confirmation. Do not apply treatments based only on this sample.

Understanding the score
